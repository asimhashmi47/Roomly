# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Employees who need a meeting room right now or later today, usually between meetings on a laptop in a bright office. Their job: see what is free, book it in a few seconds, move on. Admins (office or IT managers) also keep the room list, user accounts and system health in order.

## Product Purpose
Meeting Room Booking lets a team reserve rooms without double booking. Success is a user picking a free slot in under ten seconds and never being surprised by a clash; and, as a portfolio piece, a codebase that proves the database, not the UI, guarantees consistency.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, trustworthy. References: Linear for restraint and speed, Stripe for polish and clear state colors. Confident and quiet rather than playful.

## Anti-references
Generic CRUD admin templates; purple-to-blue gradient SaaS heroes; cards nested in cards; bouncy or decorative motion; gray-on-color text; glassmorphism as decoration.

## Design Principles
1. Free time should be obvious at a glance; the timeline is the hero of the product.
2. The server is the authority: never imply availability is guaranteed, and make a lost race feel recoverable, not like an error.
3. Familiar beats novel; standard patterns, consistent controls, motion only where it explains state.
4. Every state is designed: loading skeletons, empty states that teach, plain-language errors.
5. Small and legible: no feature that does not serve booking a room.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard operation, visible focus, labelled form fields, focus-trapped modals, and reduced-motion support on every animation. Status is never conveyed by color alone.
